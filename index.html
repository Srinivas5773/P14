HOW TO PLAY

Controls & Tips

W / UP ARROW
Accelerate Speed up and reach maximum engine speed.
S / DOWN ARROW
Brake / Reverse Slow down quickly or back up if needed.
A, D / LEFT, RIGHT
Steering & Drift Sharp turns at high speeds will slide your car into a drift.
SPACE / SHIFT
Nitro Boost Burst of high speed. Refills slowly over time or from nitro tanks.
P / ESC
Pause Pause the race to check your current stats.
R
Quick Restart Instantly restart the current race.
